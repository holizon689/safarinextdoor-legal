1. What this is

The Safari Next Door Posting Tool ("the Tool") is a private, internal tool used only by the creator of the Safari Next Door channel. It uploads the channel's own finished video clips to the creator's TikTok account as drafts. The creator reviews, edits and publishes every draft manually in the TikTok app.

2. Who can use it

The Tool is not offered to the public. Only the owner of the Safari Next Door TikTok account uses it, with their own account.

4. TikTok

Use of the Tool is also subject to TikTok's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines. The Tool uses the TikTok API only to upload drafts to the connected account and to read basic account information (display name and avatar) to confirm which account is connected.

6. Ending use

The account owner can disconnect the Tool at any time in the Tool itself or in TikTok's settings (Security and permissions > Apps and services).
